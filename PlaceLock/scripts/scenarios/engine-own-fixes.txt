# The new engine decides from its own fixes. iOS's geofence readings are pinned to "outside"
# (-geofenceReading) and its events never reach the engine, so only Cove's tracking can
# unblock and block here. This is the case where iOS never notices you arrived.
option engineMode live
option geofenceReading outside
place Gym 37.7694 -122.4862 100

at Gym +400                  # 400 m out: approach tracking
launch
expect locked
walk Gym speed 10            # arrive
expect unlock Gym within 45
expect-no lock for 30        # iOS's pinned "outside" must not undo it
walk Gym +300 speed 10       # leave
expect lock "Left Gym" within 90
expect locked
