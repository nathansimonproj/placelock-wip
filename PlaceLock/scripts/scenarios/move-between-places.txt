# Two overlapping places: walking from one into the other keeps apps open (LockRules .move),
# instead of locking on the way out of the first.
#
# Walk, don't jump: after a jump iOS may report leaving Library a moment before entering Quad,
# which locks and then unlocks again (seen 2026-11-22). iOS also only reports leaving some way
# past the edge, so Quad is big and its center is 300 m past Library's edge.
place Library 37.7694 -122.4760 100
place Quad 37.77299 -122.4760 350      # 400 m north; the circles overlap by 50 m

at 37.7640 -122.4760      # start 600 m south: Quad is to the north
launch
expect locked
at Library
expect unlock Library
walk Quad speed 10       # 400 m north: into Quad first, then out of Library
expect move within 90
expect unlocked Quad
expect-no lock for 10
at Quad +300             # leave both
expect lock "Left Quad"
expect locked
