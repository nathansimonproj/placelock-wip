Cove support

Cove keeps the apps you choose blocked everywhere except at places you save, like the gym or home. It's an iPhone app made by Nathanael Simon.

Common questions

Apps didn't unblock when I arrived

Cove needs Location set to Always with Precise Location on: iOS Settings › Cove › Location. With either one off, apps stay blocked, and Home shows what to fix. Arriving can take a minute or two to notice. Opening Cove makes it check right away.

Apps didn't block again when I left

iOS tells Cove you've left once you're a little way past the edge of the place, usually within a few minutes. Opening Cove makes it check right away.

Why does a new place say "Starts in 24 h"?

For your first 24 hours, every change applies right away so you can set things up. After that, anything that loosens the rules waits 24 hours: adding a place, moving or enlarging one, or removing a blocked app. Changes that only make things stricter apply right away. The ⓘ button at the top of Cove’s Settings tab explains it.

Home says "Blocking is off"

Cove blocks apps through Screen Time, and Screen Time access for Cove was turned off. Tap Fix on Home to turn it back on.

How do I delete my data?

In Cove, open Settings › Backup › Delete account. It deletes your backup from our server straight away. Your places and blocked apps stay on your iPhone. Details are in the privacy policy.
