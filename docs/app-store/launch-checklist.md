# Launch checklist

What's left before Spots goes on the App Store. See [Roadmap.md](../../Roadmap.md) for what's built.

## Your steps

Things only you can do (Apple account, App Store Connect, Supabase dashboard, hosting, the iPhone). One line each.

- **Check what Supabase keeps about each request:** in the Supabase dashboard, open the project › Logs (API Gateway and Auth) and Project Settings › General (region). Note whether IP addresses and user agents are kept and for how long, and add that to `docs/app-store/data-inventory.md` ("Not checked") and the privacy policy. Also check Database › Backups: how long backups keep a deleted account (the privacy policy's "How long we keep it" has a `TODO(user)` for it).
- **Finish the privacy policy:** in `docs/app-store/privacy-policy.md` fill in each `TODO(user)` (effective date, your name as it appears on the App Store, Supabase region, log and backup retention, the Children section, support email), delete the DRAFT note, then rebuild `docs/privacy/index.html` from it (same text; also delete its DRAFT banner and the `noindex` line). If you change backup to opt-in (`.night/needs-you.md`), update the Backup section first.
- **Host the privacy policy and a support page, then link them in the app:** put `docs/privacy/index.html` and a support page online (don't publish the whole `docs/` folder with GitHub Pages: it would also publish the drafts and review notes in `docs/app-store/`; copy the one file to its own Pages repo or host instead) (any `https` address, e.g. GitHub Pages), pick a support email, and fill in `privacyPolicyURL`, `supportURL` and `supportEmail` in `PlaceLock/PlaceLock/Shared/Brand.swift` (each is marked `TODO(user)`; leave one `nil` to hide its row). The privacy policy link is required by Apple. Run `PlaceLock/scripts/verify.sh --release --screens`, then open Spots › About and tap each link. Use the same privacy policy and support URLs in App Store Connect › App Information.
- **Archive and export a signed build:** with Xcode signed in to the paid team (Xcode › Settings › Accounts), run `PlaceLock/scripts/bump-build.sh` if this version's build number was already uploaded, then `PlaceLock/scripts/archive.sh`. It prints the `.ipa` path, e.g. `PlaceLock/build/archive/Spots-1.0.0-1-export/PlaceLock.ipa`.
- **Upload the build (pick one):** in Xcode, `open PlaceLock/build/archive/Spots-1.0.0-1.xcarchive`, then Organizer › Distribute App › App Store Connect › Upload; or save an app-specific password (appleid.apple.com › Sign-In and Security › App-Specific Passwords) with `xcrun altool --store-password-in-keychain-item SPOTS_ASC -u <your Apple ID> -p <app-specific password>`, then run `xcrun altool --validate-app -f PlaceLock/build/archive/Spots-1.0.0-1-export/PlaceLock.ipa -t ios -p @keychain:SPOTS_ASC` and `xcrun altool --upload-app -f PlaceLock/build/archive/Spots-1.0.0-1-export/PlaceLock.ipa -t ios -p @keychain:SPOTS_ASC`. The Transporter app (drag in the `.ipa`, click Deliver) works too.
- **Check the privacy report:** after `archive.sh` makes a signed archive, open it in Xcode's Organizer (Window › Organizer), right-click the archive › Generate Privacy Report, and check the PDF lists precise location, user ID, other user content and product interaction (linked, not tracking, app functionality) and `UserDefaults` with `CA92.1` and `1C8F.1`, matching `docs/app-store/data-inventory.md` › Privacy manifests.
- **Check that logs hide private values on the iPhone:** with the iPhone connected but no Xcode debugging, open Console on the Mac, pick the iPhone, filter on `com.nsimon.PlaceLock`, add a place and walk out of one. Place names, search text and coordinates should show as `<private>`.
- **Check the 50-website cap on the iPhone (optional):** with the paid build, Spots tab › Blocked apps › Add or remove apps, pick more than 50 websites (only sites you've opened in Safari appear), and check Spots says "Kept 50 of each" and that a kept site shows the block screen in Safari. Roadmap.md › Known issues (the app picker, question 3) has the details.
- **Keep Supabase awake while Apple reviews:** Free plan projects pause after 7 days of low activity, and then Delete account fails for the reviewer. Just before you submit, open the Supabase dashboard › the project; if it says Paused, click Resume project (or move it to the Pro plan, which never pauses). Then open Spots › Backup on the iPhone and check it says "Backed up".
- **Try Delete account on the live project:** on the iPhone, Spots › Backup › Delete account › Delete account. It should go back to the Backup screen with no "Couldn't delete your account" alert, and the Supabase dashboard › Table Editor should have no `places` or `sessions` rows for that account. App Review checks this (guideline 5.1.1(v); see `docs/app-store/review-audit.md`).
- **Fill in and enter the listing:** in `docs/app-store/listing.md` fill in each `TODO(user)` (support URL, marketing URL or empty, copyright name, a fallback name if "Spots" is taken), then in App Store Connect › the app › App Information set the name, subtitle, Productivity category and privacy policy URL, and on the 1.0 version page paste the promotional text, description and keywords. Re-run the length check at the end of `listing.md` after any edit.
- **Answer App Privacy:** App Store Connect › the app › App Privacy › Get Started, then follow `docs/app-store/app-privacy.md` (Yes; Precise Location, User ID, Other User Content, Product Interaction; each App Functionality, linked, not tracking), then Publish. Decide the Supabase IP address question in that file first.
- **Answer the age rating:** App Store Connect › the app › App Information › Age Rating › Edit, answer as in `docs/app-store/age-rating.md` (all No or None), and check it comes out 4+. Confirm "Health or Wellness Topics: No" there.
- **Pick and finish the screenshots:** look through the drafts in `.night/app-store-screens/` (read its `README.md` first), retake Home and Spots from the paid build on the iPhone so real app icons show (Xcode › Window › Devices and Simulators › Take Screenshot, or side button + volume up), then App Store Connect › the app › the 1.0 version page › Previews and Screenshots › iPhone 6.9" Display, and drag in 3–10 shots at 1320 × 2868. Screenshots from a smaller iPhone come out at its own size, which App Store Connect only takes in that size's own slot, so check the 6.9" set is all one size.
- **Record the review video:** on the iPhone with a TestFlight build, follow the script in `docs/app-store/review-notes.md` (Control Center › Screen Recording, about 60–90 s, pick two apps).
- **Fill in App Review Information:** on the 1.0 version page › App Review Information, untick sign-in required, add your name, phone and email, paste the notes from `docs/app-store/review-notes.md` (replace `Screen recording: TODO(user)`), and attach the video.
